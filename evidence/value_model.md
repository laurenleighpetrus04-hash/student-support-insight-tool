# Business / Value Model

## Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Need | Value Provided |
|---|---|---|
| Learners | Fair and timely support | Earlier identification of data, transport, academic, or mentoring needs |
| Programme staff | Clear learner support insights | Faster analysis and prioritisation |
| Tutors and mentors | Know who needs help | Better targeting of academic and mentoring support |
| Programme manager | Plan resources effectively | Evidence for support planning and reporting |
| Institution | Improve learner success | Better learner retention and programme quality |

## Value Proposition

The Student Support Insights Tool helps programme staff turn learner support survey data into practical insights. Instead of manually reading a spreadsheet, staff can upload a CSV file, validate the data, view visual trends, identify support-risk learners, and generate recommendations.

## Service Model

1. Learners complete a support survey.
2. Staff export the responses as a CSV file.
3. Staff upload the CSV into the prototype.
4. The tool validates and analyses the dataset.
5. Programme staff review the insights.
6. Support actions are planned for learner groups or individual learners.

## Decision-Making Support

The tool supports better decision-making by making support needs visible, identifying high and medium risk learners earlier, and producing consistent recommendations. It does not replace staff judgement.
