# To-Be Learner Support Process

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Learner survey data collected] --> B[CSV uploaded into Student Support Insights Tool]
    B --> C[Tool validates data]
    C --> D[Dashboard shows learner support trends]
    D --> E[Tool flags high and medium support-risk learners]
    E --> F[Tool generates insights and recommendations]
    F --> G[Programme staff review findings]
    G --> H[Support actions planned and monitored]
```

## Improvements

1. Faster analysis of learner survey data.
2. Better data quality through missing value, invalid value, and duplicate checks.
3. Clearer visibility of support needs through dashboard visualisations.
4. Earlier identification of learners needing support.
5. Human review remains part of the process before any action is taken.
